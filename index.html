02 / The person behind the pixels

Candela, behind the scenes.

I'm Candela, a 3D artist drawn to tactile details, unexpected colors, and objects that feel almost alive. I turn small observations into playful visual worlds, from the first sketch to the final render.

Away from the viewport, you'll find me filling sketchbooks, taking photos, and finding inspiration in the ordinary. Having worked in hospitality as a waitress, I've developed a sharp eye for pacing, adaptability, and understanding how people interact with their environment.
